VCP Swing Plan — TSX Daily Charts

Session prepared for: Tuesday, September 8, 2026 (TSX closed Monday Sept 7 — Labour Day) · Account $5,000 CAD · Risk per trade $100 (2%) · Max 2 open positions · No margin

VCP = Volatility Contraction Pattern (Minervini): successive price pullbacks shrink and volume dries up into the pivot. Educational template — no security is named or recommended here; candidates come from your own scan in §2.

Mon eve scan + shortlist + write orders Tue 9:15 news check on your names Tue 9:30 stop-limit orders work; you don't watch Tue 4:10pm confirm fills + GTC stops, journal Nightly manage per §5

1 · The setup — every box must tick

  • Trend: price above a rising 50-day MA; 50 > 150 > 200-day; within 25% of the 52-week high; stronger than the TSX Composite over the last 3 months.
  • Base: 3–8 weeks sideways after an advance, with 2–4 contractions, each pullback roughly half the previous (e.g. 18% → 10% → 5%). Final contraction ≤ 10% deep and tight for 3+ days.
  • Volume: shrinking through the base; final days clearly below the 50-day average (the dry-up). No heavy red distribution days inside the final third.
  • Pivot: one clean resistance level across the final tight zone. The breakout itself must run on volume ≥ 1.5× the 50-day average — a quiet breakout is a fade, not a buy.
  • TSX liquidity gates: main board only (no Venture/CSE at this account size); price ≥ $5; average daily dollar-volume ≥ $1M.

2 · Monday evening — finding candidates (45 min)

  • Screen (TradingView, or your broker's screener): exchange = TSX · price ≥ $5 · avg volume × price ≥ $1M · within 25% of 52-wk high · price > 50-day MA > 200-day MA. Expect a few dozen names.
  • Eyeball daily charts for the contraction staircase and volume dry-up of §1. Shortlist ≤ 5, then keep the best two at most.
  • For each: mark the pivot, the stop (below the final contraction low), compute shares (§3), and pre-log the plan in the toolkit's Trade Journal.
  • If nothing qualifies, nothing is traded Tuesday. "Stand down" is this plan's most common correct output — the edge is in the waiting.

3 · Position size — your numbers, precomputed

  • Formula: shares = floor( ($100 − round-trip fees) ÷ (entry − stop) ). Use a $0-commission broker at this size — a $10 round trip burns 10% of the risk budget (83 shares becomes 75 in the example below).
  • Worked example (hypothetical prices): pivot $20.00, stop $18.80 → risk $1.20/share → 83 shares ≈ $1,660, i.e. ~33% of the account. Max loss if stopped: $99.60 ✓.
  • At $5,000, positions of 25–40% of the account are normal and fine — the stop, not diversification, is the risk control. Hard caps: 2 open positions; same-sector names count as one; never margin.
  • $100 = 2% risk is the aggressive end of standard practice. Until 30 logged trades prove the edge, $50 (1%) is the professional choice — same plan, half the tuition.

4 · Entry — Tuesday, Sept 8 protocol

  • Order: buy stop-limit — trigger at pivot + $0.05–0.10, limit at pivot + ~0.5%. Day order. The break of the pivot is the only entry; never buy inside the base "for a better price."
  • Post-long-weekend gap rule: three days of news reopen with the market. Gap open > 2% above the pivot → cancel, don't chase; wait for a new tight entry later in the week. Gap down through the planned stop → setup void.
  • 9:15 ET (5 min): check your one or two names for weekend news, earnings dates, halts — anything material → cancel that order.
  • 9:30–9:45: opening auction noise; the stop-limit does the filtering. No market orders, no watching.
  • On fill: the GTC stop-loss goes in at the broker immediately — before anything else. Set a price alert, close the app, go to work.

5 · Managing it — after entry

  • Initial stop: below the final contraction low, typically 4–8% under the pivot. A structure needing a wider stop is a pass, not a wider stop. GTC, at the broker, never mental, never moved down.
  • Failed breakout: a close back inside the base on heavy volume → exit at the next open. Don't wait to be stopped; failure is information.
  • Profits: at +2R (+$200) sell half; trail the remainder under the 10-day low. Time stop: less than +1R after 10 sessions → exit. Capital at $5,000 is too scarce to idle.
  • Weekly circuit breaker: −2R (−$200) in a calendar week → flat and no new orders until the weekend review.

6 · Review — where this plan is allowed to change

  • Journal every trade (and every valid setup you skipped, with why) in the toolkit the day it happens — entry, stop, exit, R-multiple, screenshots if you can.
  • Weekend review: stats vs. the Phase-1 gate — 30+ trades, positive expectancy after costs, rules followed on ≥ 90% of trades.
  • Rules change only at 30-trade reviews. Never mid-trade, never mid-week, never because one trade hurt. One trade means nothing; thirty begin to mean something.
